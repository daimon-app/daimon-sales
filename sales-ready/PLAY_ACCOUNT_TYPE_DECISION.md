# PLAY ACCOUNT TYPE DECISION

Verified against official Google Play Help on 2026-08-21.

| | Personal | Organization |
|---|---|---|
| Intended use | personal, student, hobbyist | organization or business activity |
| Monetization/features | supported | supported |
| D-U-N-S | not required | required |
| New-account testing | accounts created after 2023-11-13 require 12 opted-in testers for 14 continuous days before Production access | the cited new-personal-account gate does not apply as the personal rule |
| Identity consequence | permanent owner identity; verification required | legal organization data must match D-U-N-S; verification required |
| Fastest if no D-U-N-S exists | usually Personal, but testing adds at least 14 days | D-U-N-S acquisition may take up to 30 days |

Official sources:

- Account type: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634885?hl=en
- Registration/D-U-N-S: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312?hl=en
- Personal account testing: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en
- Data Safety: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en
- Payments policy: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en

Decision remains Owner-only. Do not infer organization status from product ambition alone.
