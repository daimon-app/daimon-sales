# DAIMON repository boundary

## Public development asset

The root `index.html`, `manifest.json`, `sw.js`, and root `assets/` are the historical three-mode PWA development asset. They are public development/reference material and are **not** the paid `DAIMON MORNING Edition`, its Android source, its Play artifact, or its customer support surface.

Do not package, list, advertise, or submit the root PWA as `DAIMON MORNING Edition`. Do not infer paid-product functionality from WORK/NIGHT content in the public PWA.

## Paid product source

The paid Morning-only Android source of truth is the private Repository `daimon-app/daimon-morning-sales`:

- applicationId: `app.daimon.morning`
- product: Morning only
- release delivery: Google Play signed AAB
- WORK/NIGHT/legacy PWA: excluded

Keystores, passwords, signed artifacts, and paid-product source must not be copied into this public Repository.

## Public sales material

Public launch preparation lives in `marketing/`. Its release gates remain authoritative for LP, Play assets, CM, SNS, legal/support, E2E, and owner approvals. Staging screenshots and videos explicitly marked `staging-blocked` are not publishable product evidence.
