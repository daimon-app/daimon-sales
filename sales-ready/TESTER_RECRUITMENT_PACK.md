# DAIMON tester recruitment pack

Status: `TEMPLATE READY / NO PARTICIPANTS RECRUITED`

## Recruitment copy

DAIMON Android 4-mode release candidate testers are invited to verify installation, first launch, Morning/Work/Night/Adversity navigation, audio stop behavior, offline bundled content, restart/persistence, and Google Play test-purchase/restore behavior. Participation is voluntary; no benefit or medical outcome is promised.

## Form fields

- Consent to tester privacy notice (required)
- Email address (required; encrypted non-Git storage)
- Android device model and OS version
- Availability for Play test-track installation
- Audio test availability (optional)
- Accessibility needs (optional; do not collect diagnosis)
- Consent to receive invitation/follow-up (required)

Never collect passwords, OTPs, payment credentials, purchase tokens, government IDs or unnecessary sensitive data.

## Test flow

1. Owner approves final invite list; participant opts in.
2. Tester receives Play opt-in/install instructions.
3. Record app version/device/OS, not account secrets.
4. Run first launch, four modes, Back/Home, audio/background, airplane mode, restart, update and purchase restoration.
5. Report expected/actual result, severity, reproduction steps and optional redacted screenshot.
6. End participation on request and delete contact data under the approved retention policy.
